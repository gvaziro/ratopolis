JUDGE 0 (winner=juice): I judged mainly on moment-to-moment fun and pacing, and 'juice' wins on that. It is the only design where every second has tactile feedback: an arcing loot flight, a readiness ring, chambers that visibly grow through tiers, and stray rats arriving at 10 crumbs. Its train is the most satisfying version of the brief's hook: swipe-collect, Peggle-style rising combo pitch, a skill-based grappling-hook jackpot with slow-mo, and a lantern greed trade-off. Its overflow rule (event loot ignores the cap and never burns) protects the big payoff that 'economy' undermines by letting the overflow melt. Automation arrives every 10-20 minutes and harmful events wait until the player is active, which respects idle play. It also ships with simulator checkpoints and a P0/P1/P2 list, so it is more buildable than 'theme'.

'theme' has the best world and the best two-scale presentation: Human sees / Rats see lines, the one-Tuesday clock, and the City Above flip. Its scope is the largest and its first run the slowest, with real risk of event fatigue. 'economy' has the most rigorous systems (bottleneck hints, value-weighted prestige, crew position choice, the early pneumatic capsule) but weaker feel and some anti-satisfying rules.

Recommended graft onto 'juice':
- From 'economy': the early pneumatic-capsule mini-train to bring the hook forward from minute 49, converter bottleneck hints, and the crew position risk choice.
- From 'theme': Human/Rat lines on every event, train rating tiers, x2 level milestones, lane nets as an optional prep layer, and the City Above sky-strip flip as a stretch goal.

JUDGE 1 (winner=juice): I scored each design on implementability, data-drivenness and risk, and checked the arithmetic in each document by hand.

**juice wins (32).** It is the design that can actually ship this session:
- **Build plan:** it alone gives a P0/P1/P2 cut list.
- **Data-driven content:** it has a full effect-type taxonomy, a single unlock-condition mini-language, and simulator checkpoints with a fixed tuning order.
- **Fewest stall risks:**
  - Event loot overflows the cap without decay.
  - Expeditions and the Great Engine are contribution projects that caps do not block.
  - Hunger only slows production.
  - Harmful events require the player to be present.
- **Numbers hold up:** the births timing, crown formula and Engine sums all check out.
- **Best moment-to-moment feel:** swipe collecting, combo with rising pitch, the carriage tutorial at 3:30, and a first train with ×3 loot.
- **Risks:** too many bespoke minigames (valves, rhythm, hook), the suspicion system, and caps on late coin costs that nobody has validated. The P0 list contains most of this.

**economy is close behind (31).** It has the most rigorous numbers: the chain ratios really are integer and they check out. Its seeded step() function and idle/active bot windows make a better simulator, and one shared falling-items component handles every event. It loses on:
- single-payment zone projects that must fit the cap (stall risk);
- a decaying overflow state;
- fame that double-counts intermediates;
- a late flagship train;
- no priority cuts.

**theme has the strongest hook (10 on two-scale) but the riskiest scope (30).** It has the best event architecture (phase primitives declared in data) and the cheapest theme wins: the 'Человек видит / Крысы видят' line on every event and the human clock. But it brings 8 events, faction ranks, a train prep panel and a 3.5-hour first run, and leaves cap gating on the wonder unspecified.

**Recommended synthesis:** build on juice's tables and P0 list, then graft in:
- **From economy:**
  - integer chain ratios with the bottleneck hint;
  - the speed vs yield effect split;
  - the seeded RNG and the two simulator bots;
  - the pneumatic-capsule mini-train around 20 min, so the hook comes earlier;
  - one falling-items component;
  - converters that pause when their output is at cap;
  - starvation that spares foragers;
  - a 20 s gap between events.
- **From theme:**
  - the event-phase primitive schema;
  - two-scale text lines for every event;
  - EMA30-based rewards;
  - keeping 📐 and 💍 out of global multipliers;
  - train rating tiers;
  - the 'К путям!' toast;
  - autoEff for unattended events;
  - the human clock and newspaper as cheap flavor.

JUDGE 2 (winner=juice): 'juice' is the best base for the brief as written. Its five zones are exactly the requested ones (нора у стока → канализация → подвалы → станция метро → вокзал), and its starting loot is the requested crumbs, coins, buttons and wire. It also makes the two-scale hook felt rather than described. The train scene has the most tactile hooks: 1.6×-window wheels, a joint rhythm, swipe-collect, a combo with rising pitch, a greed lantern, and a grappling-hook jackpot. Its P0/P1/P2 plan keeps it shippable.

'theme' has the strongest fantasy ideas but is the biggest scope risk, with shakier food and growth balance. Its best ideas should be grafted onto the juice base:
- the human clock ('one Tuesday is a rat epoch')
- per-event 👤/🐀 lines
- the train prep phase with nets, shelter and daredevils
- harvesters and narrow-gauge carts clearing piles
- flipping the strip in the finale

'economy' should contribute its brass→gears→rails→clockwork chain, so the miniature railway and the industrial empire become real. It should also contribute the Rat Express that rats build from junk as the finale, the early pneumatic-capsule mini-train to bring the train loop forward, the crew-position risk choice, and its simulator acceptance windows.
